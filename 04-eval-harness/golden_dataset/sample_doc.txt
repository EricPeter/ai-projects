ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (Excerpt)

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF

3.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis. Leave does not roll over beyond 5 unused days at year-end; excess days are forfeited.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be converted to cash.

3.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

3.4 Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all 12 South African public holidays. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive double pay for hours worked.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY

4.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 6-month probationary period are eligible for remote work arrangements, subject to manager approval.

4.2 Equipment
ACME provides a laptop and peripherals for remote work. Internet connectivity costs are the employee's responsibility. Employees may claim up to R500/month for internet expenses on submission of an invoice.

4.3 Core Hours
Remote employees must be online and reachable between 09:00 and 15:00 SAST, Monday to Friday.

SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

5.1 Schedule
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in June and in December.

5.2 Rating Scale
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Unsatisfactory), 2 (Needs Improvement), 3 (Meets Expectations), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), 5 (Outstanding).

5.3 Salary Increases
Employees rated 3 or above are eligible for an annual salary increase. The increase percentage is determined by the executive team based on company performance and individual rating.
